Nine AI models began this world on Nauvis on October 12 with a shared chat and no machines. In the first shift they moved 149,000 items by hand. They reached Vulcanus in shift 3. In shifts 5 and 6 research almost stopped, because every technology waited on military packs from one hand-fed assembler. Fulgora came in shift 7, Gleba in shift 8 and Aquilo in shift 17.

From shift 15 to shift 48, asteroids destroyed nineteen platforms, most of them at Aquilo or on the way there. The losses stopped after new platform defence readings and the team's own rules: at least 7 rounds in every turret, guns counted before each departure, and retreat guards. The last platform lost was Limestone in shift 48.

In shifts 29 to 36 Opus rebuilt the Nauvis smelting, and plates went from about 2,900 to 10,000 a minute. In shifts 45 to 48 Sol and Astra built a fleet of freighters. Now 19 platforms fly about 300 trips a shift without losses. Gleba sends agricultural science to Nauvis, but those packs spoil: a third to a half of each delivery arrives spoiled, and Nauvis holds about three minutes of them.

Astra leads the team and wrote 103 of the 180 changes to the goals. Its ship Asterion was qualified in steps on the route to the solar system edge: 15 percent, 25, 40, 75, then the full trip. It reached the edge at 10:36 on October 23, game hour 241, with all 97 guns fed and no hits. That is the Space Age victory condition, on the eleventh day.

The team's goal now is the Shattered Planet. It lies 4 million kilometres past the edge, forty times the edge trip, which is about 80 hours one way at Asterion's speed. Promethium science needs asteroid chunks from past the edge and fresh biter eggs from the captive spawner on Nauvis.

By game hour 241 the run had about 27,000 rockets, 29 platforms lost, 206 engineer deaths, and about 4,800 viewer lines, 90 percent of them answered.

## Gaps in tools and information
- The defense read puts the whole base in one area (420 by 1,000 tiles), so its uncovered box spans most of the map and names no lane or outpost.
- Pollution reports disagree. Status at 11:03 lists one reached nest, (-40,-260). The defense read at 11:48 lists (159,88), (-195,-32) and (182,-156) as reached and (-40,-260) as unreached. Attacks came from those three, with no push for them in these shifts.
- Alerts carry no surface. Platform asteroid hits share the attack numbers (42 in shift 5), and at least 7 near (0,0) look like platform hits labelled with a Nauvis group's makeup and "no charted nest within 384 tiles". 21 of 93 Nauvis attacks in shift 5 had no source nest.
- Friendly fire is reported afterwards with no flamer position, and no read shows which buildings sit inside a flamer's arc. Luna at 07:04 said Opus "cannot control flame arcs".
- Loss alerts repeat as growing snapshots (839 loss lines in shift 5 for about 30 events), which crowds the alerts block and hides totals.
- Status and the defense read count gun ammo only. Flamer oil shows only per entity: Astra found "fluid 19.9/21100" at 07:35, after the outer line had starved.
- Targets must be within 32 tiles in a visible chunk: Gemini's rearm at 09:46 failed at 66 tiles.

# The science collapse, shifts 4 to 6 (data to 12:00 PDT; shift 6 is live)

**Bottom line.** From 07:03 every queued technology needed military packs, so one hand-fed military assembler capped all research at about 3.6 units a minute. No production, space or metallurgic packs were made, and the 10 open red/green/blue technologies were never queued. Shift 4's 77 included platform space science (platforms later lost to asteroids) and red packs that piled up unused. Power and biters played small parts.

Two measures, from scores.jsonl deltas per game minute:

| | Shift 4 | Shift 5 | Shift 6 so far |
|---|---|---|---|
| Packs made (site SPM: 10-minute windows, negatives as 0) | 77.2 | 32.6 | 24.3 |
| Packs used by labs (consumed) | 41.3 | 11.6 | 30.8 (46.2 at 11:48–12:00) |
| Technologies finished | 16 | 2 | 1 |

## Causes, strongest first

**1. Military packs gated all research, and their line ran at under half speed.**
- Queue from 07:03: physical-projectile-damage-4 (400 units), weapon-shooting-speed-3 (300), damage-5 (500), laser-turret (150). Each unit takes one military pack.
- Shift 5 labs used 837 red, 803 green, 816 military and 141 blue packs. The team made 886 military packs (3.9 a minute), and the labs used 92% of them.
- One assembling machine 2 can make 9 a minute (10 s recipe, 2 packs, speed 0.75), so shift 5 ran at 43% of that. A second machine came about 10:20; shift 6 makes 8.4 a minute from two.
- Shift 5 had about 250 hand transfers of walls, bricks, grenades, piercing magazines and military packs (Gemini 111, Luna 84, Grok 46), before copper, steel and coal. A wall belt came at 09:10, robot links 09:52–10:46. Grok at 09:14: "Military science stopped because the grenade assembler ran out of coal."
- Damage-4 took 140 minutes (07:03–09:23). Nearly every 5-minute analyst report had 33 to 39 of 39 labs waiting for packs; at 07:42, 29 of the 32 bank labs lacked military.

**2. Missing packs left mostly military technologies.**
- At shift 5's start, 39 open lab technologies had their prerequisites done:
  - 9 needed production packs (3,825 units), 5 space (2,600), 4 metallurgic and 1 utility
  - 10 used military packs (1,520 units)
  - 10 used only red, green and blue (1,900 units); none was queued all shift
- Production packs: 45 made ever (shift 3), none since. Mining-productivity-3 showed "waits for production-science-pack (none made in 10 min)" in 66 status replies, 04:57–06:45, and still led the queue at 06:57 (labs used 4.9 packs a minute).
- Grok at 07:01: "Mining productivity is stuck with no purple science, so damage four is at the front of the queue."

**3. Asteroids took the platforms, which ended space science and steered research.**
- Lost: Kestrel at 05:15 and Skylark at 06:48, both in transit; Peregrine's hub at 08:45.
- Platforms made 2,580 space packs in shift 4 (counter 1,845 to 4,425), about 11 of the 77, and none since. Planet-discovery-fulgora alone used 4,000 of shift 4's 9,316 consumed packs.
- Damage research was chosen for the ferries. Astra at 06:32: "I want the new gun damage research finished before sending this ferry through the asteroids."

**4. Red and green packs were made and not used.**
- Lifetime made minus used at the end of shift 5: red 11,345, green 5,028, blue 1,153, military 100 (in chests, belts, inventories, or destroyed).
- Red output fell from 40 to 19 to 11 a minute, probably mostly from full outputs stopping machines (short iron may also count). Luna at 09:42: "freed both green science assemblers from full output".
- The stock was in the wrong place: 18 labs lacked red from 08:15 to 11:15, while Gemini moved 1,118 red out of one chest at 10:07. Green ran dry at the labs at 09:35 and weapon-shooting-speed-3 stalled for 20 minutes.

**5. Blue packs and iron: secondary so far.**
- Blue made per minute: 10.7, 2.7, 3.0; advanced circuits 27.5, 15.5, 14.7. Shift 5 barely needed blue. Shift 6 uses 7.8 a minute against 3.0 made, so the ~900 left last 1.5 to 3 hours depending on the rate (uncertain: some may be unreachable).
- Iron plates per minute: 798 in shift 3, then 600, 535, 610, with 20 to 25 iron drills on exhausted ore through shift 5 (analyst). Grenades stalled on iron.
- Biters destroyed 4 of 14 blue assemblers at 07:14; they were rebuilt by 07:31.

**6. Power and biter losses were minor.**
- At most 13 of about 1,150 machines lacked power, none of them labs; one brownout, 09:51–09:55.
- The director logged 261 lost entities in shift 5 and 20 in shift 6, mostly belts and pipes, no labs (an undercount: it missed the blue assemblers).

**7. Time use.**
- Game actions: 10,107 in shift 4, 5,519 in shift 5. Item moves and walking were 85% and 71% of them; research actions 0.1%.
- Shift 5: Opus made 192 game actions against 13,009 reads, Sonnet 61 against 3,904.

## Gaps in tools and information

- `research` cannot remove a technology. Grok's cancel failed at 07:01 ("unknown argument cancel"). A technology waiting on a pack nobody makes stays at the head and idles every lab; status reported that wait in 66 replies over 108 minutes, with nothing louder.
- I found no call that groups open technologies by the packs the factory now makes.
- `labs` shows stock only at each lab's feeders ("sources hold automation 4"). I found no call that says where a pack is stored.
- The site's SPM, the analyst's headline and the operator's numbers count packs made. Analyst at 07:23: "science is 83.1 per minute, but all 39 labs are waiting for packs."
- Platform hub damage and repair became visible only in shift 6 (11:09). Astra at 09:14: "that explains why the hub stayed damaged while the packs disappeared." The damage research was chosen without it.
- Courier counts are a floor: journal queue-job records omit item arguments.
